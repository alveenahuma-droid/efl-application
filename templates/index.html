How a conversion works

  1. Enter the amount

    Use a figure from 300 to 5,000 in the currency you are selling.

  2. Choose both currencies

    GBP, USD, EUR, BRL, JPY, and TRY are available. The two sides must differ.

  3. Review the result

    You see the rate, the fee, and the amount you receive. The conversion is kept in your history.
